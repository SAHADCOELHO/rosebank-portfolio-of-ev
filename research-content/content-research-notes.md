# LUMILA — Content research and sourcing notes

## Project status
LUMILA is treated as a fictional academic business concept because no verified organisation profile was supplied.

## Proposed content areas
- Organisation overview: fictional online retailer for the Angolan market.
- Mission and vision: draft statements created for this project.
- Target audience: young adults and working customers who browse and enquire using mobile devices.
- Product categories: fashion, accessories, lifestyle and gifts. These are illustrative categories, not verified inventory.
- Contact details: not supplied; therefore no address, telephone number, email address or map pin is invented.

## Asset sourcing
The first version uses an original SVG banner made for this assignment. No third-party photographs are embedded. If external images are added in later parts, record the image title, creator, source URL, licence and access date. Check any separate rights relating to identifiable people, brands, logos or artwork.
